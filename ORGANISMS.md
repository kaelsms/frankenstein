# organism comparison

## C. elegans
- 302 neurons total
- ~6,400 chemical synapses, ~900 gap junctions
- fully mapped since 1986 (White et al.)
- robust chemotaxis, thermotaxis, escape behavior
- limited associative learning capacity

## Drosophila melanogaster
- 139,255 neurons (FlyWire, 2024)
- 54.5 million synaptic connections
- 8,400+ cell types
- mushroom body: associative memory, dopamine-gated Hebbian learning
- central complex: heading integration, path integration, steering
- complete adult brain mapped 2024
