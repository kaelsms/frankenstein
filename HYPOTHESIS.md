# hypothesis

the organizational principles of nervous systems are modular and composable across species boundaries.

## predictions

1. a hybrid system combining worm sensorimotor circuits with fly associative circuits will produce adaptive behavior that neither generates alone

2. the worm's clean sensorimotor pipeline can serve as an effector for the fly's decision-making circuits

3. context-dependent motor responses will emerge when fly learning circuits modulate worm motor output

## null hypothesis

hybrid activity statistics are a simple superposition of the two parent systems with no emergent properties.

## falsifiability

if experiments 1-4 all show null results (hybrid = superposition), the compositional modularity hypothesis is rejected for this species pair and circuit selection.

if experiment 4 specifically shows no conflict resolution behavior, the bridge layer design is insufficient for cross-species integration at the behavioral level.
