# reading list

- White et al. 1986 — C. elegans nervous system structure
- Varshney et al. 2011 — C. elegans neuronal network structural properties
- Cook et al. 2019 — whole-animal connectomes of both C. elegans sexes
- Dorkenwald et al. 2024 — neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain (FlyWire)
- Schlegel et al. 2024 — whole-brain annotation and multi-connectome cell typing
- Aso et al. 2014 — mushroom body neuronal architecture
- Seelig & Jayaraman 2015 — neural dynamics for landmark orientation
